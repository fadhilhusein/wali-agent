# Message motion and scrolling audit

Mode: after (session choice). Scope: user-requested softer bubble appearance and smooth scrolling.

No actionable findings remain in the checked scope. New user/assistant/status rows use a restrained 240 ms fade and 8 px transform. Existing messages are captured on mounting and do not replay their entry animation when returning to Chat. Semantic list/status structure and financial content remain unchanged.

Native smooth scrolling follows messages when appropriate. A programmatic-scroll marker prevents intermediate scroll events from stopping it prematurely. User wheel-up, touch/pointer interaction and upward navigation keys interrupt it. Older-message reading is preserved, sending resumes following, and reduced motion uses instant updates. The empty welcome area is not automatically scrolled to the bottom.

Mock tests verify actual animation durations and intermediate scroll positions, reading interruption, follow resumption, reduced motion, existing history without replay and browser errors. Full Chat and entrance tests, typecheck, lint, format check and production build pass. No real finance/model actions are used; physical device scroll momentum is not claimed as tested.
